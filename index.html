STEPPED-UP BASIS — Crypto Inheritance Tax Advantage
Under current US law, when you die and pass assets to heirs, the cost basis resets to fair market value at date of death. Heirs can sell immediately with zero capital gains tax regardless of how much the asset appreciated during your lifetime.
EXAMPLE
You bought BTC at $30K. Worth $140K at death. Heir's basis = $140K. They sell at $140K. They owe $0. Your lifetime gain of $110K/BTC passes completely tax-free.
RISK FLAG
This law could change. Legislative discussions have proposed eliminating stepped-up basis for large estates. Monitor tax law changes annually. Consult a CPA for your specific situation.
